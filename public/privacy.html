Privacy Policy

Effective: May 27, 2026

Terenc, LLC ("we," "us") operates ALLDEEZMeals. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

You provide:

  • Email address — for magic-link sign-in. We do not store passwords.
  • Display name and nearest ALDI store — provided at signup.
  • App content you create — saved recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, staples/pantry items, food preferences (liked/avoided), and any order or receipt details you submit for tracking.

Collected automatically:

  • Basic technical data needed to run and secure the Service (e.g., standard server logs from our hosting provider).
  • Local storage / cookies used only to keep you signed in and to save your in-app planning state. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use your information

To authenticate you; to provide meal planning, recipe generation, grocery lists, and nutrition estimates; to save your plans and preferences across sessions; to operate, secure, and improve the Service; and to communicate with you about your account.

3. Service providers / subprocessors

We share the minimum necessary with providers that help run the Service:

Provider Purpose Data involved
Supabase Authentication + database hosting Account info, app content
Vercel Application hosting / delivery Technical/log data
Anthropic Generates recipe suggestions Your planning inputs (e.g., headcount, cuisine, preferences) sent to produce a meal
USDA FoodData Central Nutrition lookups Ingredient/product names
Open Food Facts Nutrition lookups Ingredient/product identifiers

Instacart: sending a list to Instacart is a user-initiated text hand-off. We do not transmit your data to Instacart; your use of Instacart is governed by Instacart's own terms and privacy policy.

4. What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and app content while your account is active. If you request deletion, we remove your account data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain limited information to comply with legal obligations or secure the Service.

6. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your plans and preferences in the app. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data — or to close your account — email alldeezmeals@terenc.com. We will verify your request against your account email before acting.

7. Security

Sign-in is passwordless (email magic link). Privileged data operations run server-side under restricted credentials, not in your browser. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

8. Children

The Service is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact alldeezmeals@terenc.com and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here with a new effective date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: alldeezmeals@terenc.com.
