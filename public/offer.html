Beta Offer — 1 Year Free

Updated: June 5, 2026

The short version: The first 50 qualifying beta testers get one full year free on the paid plan when it launches. To qualify: generate a weekly meal plan with at least 5 dinners and share feedback.

What is the offer?

The first 50 qualifying beta testers will receive one year free on the ALLDEEZMeals paid plan when it launches — at whatever price the paid plan is set to at launch. No credit card needed now. No action required at launch beyond redeeming with the email you used to sign up.

How do I qualify?

You qualify by meeting both of the following conditions:

  1. Generate a weekly plan with at least 5 dinners. Use the app to plan and accept 5 or more dinners in a single week.
  2. Provide feedback. Send feedback about your experience — bugs, ideas, what worked, what didn't. Email alldeezmeals@terenc.com, reply to any email you receive from us, or fill out a feedback form when we send one.

Both conditions must be met. One or the other alone is not enough.

When does the free year start?

The clock starts when the paid plan launches — not when you qualify. Qualifying now locks in your spot; you don't start burning free time until there's actually a paid plan to use.

Is there a limit?

Yes — 50 slots total. Slots are claimed in the order beta testers qualify (both conditions met). Once all 50 slots are filled, this offer closes. We'll announce when that happens.

One redemption per person

One free year per verified email address. This offer can't be transferred, combined with other discounts, or redeemed more than once per account.

What if the paid plan never launches?

If the paid plan never launches or ALLDEEZMeals shuts down, no one owes anything. This offer is contingent on a paid plan existing. Qualifying during beta creates no financial obligation on either side.

What counts as "feedback"?

Anything useful: a bug report, a feature request, a quick note on whether a generated recipe actually worked out, or a reply to one of our check-in emails. We're not looking for an essay — just genuine, first-hand input from using the app.

How will I know I've qualified?

We'll send a confirmation email to the address on your account once we've logged both conditions as met. If you're not sure whether your feedback was received, email alldeezmeals@terenc.com and we'll check.

Questions?

Email alldeezmeals@terenc.com.
